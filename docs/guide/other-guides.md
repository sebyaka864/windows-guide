# other guides

useful guides, wikis, and projects for when your next rabbit hole is not windows.

## pc setup and troubleshooting

- [moddinglinked setup](https://moddinglinked.com/setup.html) — game setup and modding guidance.
- [gillian guide](https://gillian-guide.github.io/) — a practical pc guide.
- [rtech support](https://rtech.support/) — community troubleshooting and pc advice.
- [viren's guides](https://guides.viren070.me/) — concise technical guides.

## learning and discovery

- [build your own x](https://github.com/codecrafters-io/build-your-own-x) — tutorials for rebuilding familiar technology from scratch.
- [fmhy](https://fmhy.net/) — a large community-maintained internet resource index.

## games and hardware

- [consolemods wiki](https://consolemods.org/wiki/Main_Page) — console modding, repair, and restoration information.
- [emulation general wiki](https://emulation.gametechwiki.com/index.php/Main_Page) — emulator and compatibility information.
- [java arguments guide](https://exa.y2k.diy/garden/jvm-args/) — a guide to jvm arguments.
- [managing java](https://notes.highlysuspect.agency/blog/managing_java/) — notes on installing and managing java versions.
- [sim racing guide](https://www.briankoponen.com/simracing.html) — sim racing setup resources.
- [modbo ps2 modchip guide](https://quade.co/ps2-modchip-guide/modbo/) — a playstation 2 modchip guide.
- [cleanroom](https://cleanroommc.com/) — minecraft modding tools and documentation.

:::caution use your judgment
these are external resources maintained by other people. read their instructions carefully, use legitimate copies of games and software, and back up your device before changing it.
:::
