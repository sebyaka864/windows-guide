# other guides

useful guides, wikis, and projects for when your next rabbit hole is not windows.

### 📁 [Awesome Piracy](https://shakil-shahadat.github.io/awesome-piracy/)

- Compilation of secure links to legitimate websites for resources related to sailing the seas.

### 📁 [Champagne Piracy Wiki](https://champagne.pages.dev/)

- Safeguarded links spanning a multitude of platforms and categories.

### 📁 [EverythingMoe](https://everythingmoe.com/)

- Anime streaming information including websites, apps, news, and more.

### 📁 [FMHY](https://fmhy.net/)

- A huge collection of free resources from across the internet.

### 📁 [MediaSavvy](https://mediasavvy.pages.dev/)

- A versatile wiki offering a wide range of media options.

### 📁 [Ripped](https://ripped.guide/)

### 📁 [The Megathread](https://rentry.co/megathread)

- A broad collection of online resources.

### 📁 [Wiki Index](https://wiki-index.pages.dev/)

- A directory of free resources and community-maintained wikis.

### 📁 [Wotaku](https://wotaku.wiki/)

- Resources covering Japanese media and language.
